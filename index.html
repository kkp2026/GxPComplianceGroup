About the Consortium

Practical, risk-based GxP support — grounded in how inspections actually run

We are a global consortium of senior GxP professionals specializing in inspection readiness, audits, and compliance strategy across GMP, GCP, and GLP. With deep, hands-on experience facing regulators such as FDA, Health Canada, EMA, PMDA, and MFDS, we help organizations walk into inspections fully prepared.

Our approach delivers end-to-end support — from audit planning to CAPA closure and effectiveness verification — for pharmaceutical, biotech, medical device, and CRO organizations.

Core Capabilities

Three integrated pillars of GxP excellence

Offered as standalone engagements or as a fully integrated GMP–GCP–GLP program tailored to your pipeline and risk profile.

PILLAR 01

GCP Auditing & Clinical Quality

Clinical Quality & GCP Auditing

End-to-end clinical audit, monitoring, and inspection-readiness support for sponsors, CROs, and clinical sites across all phases.

Audits

Clinical sites (Phase 1–4), vendors, CROs, CMO/CDMOs, TMF/eTMF, study reports (clinical & nonclinical), and sponsor / system / process audits — monitoring, data management, statistics, pharmacovigilance, and protocol.

Regulatory Inspections

Inspection readiness, preparation, and management; mock pre-approval and routine audits aligned to FDA, EMA, PMDA, MFDS, and Health Canada expectations; SME training; post-inspection agency communication.

Clinical Operations

Site qualification, initiation, routine monitoring, and close-out visits; site personnel training; regulatory and ethics submissions; development and review of clinical protocols, investigator's brochures, informed consent, eCRFs, data management plans, pharmacy manuals, and statistical analysis plans.

Quality Management

SOPs, training programs, risk management, deviation handling, CAPA, change management, validation, and QA process design.

PILLAR 02

GMP Auditing & Manufacturing Compliance

Manufacturing Compliance & cGMP

cGMP audit, validation, and inspection support across drug, biologic, nutraceutical, cosmeceutical, and medical device operations.

Audits

Manufacturing facilities (drug, biologic, nutraceuticals, cosmeceuticals, medical devices), CMOs / CDMOs, API suppliers, packaging and labeling operations, distribution centers, laboratories, and validation providers.

Regulatory Inspections

Inspection readiness, preparation, and management; mock inspections aligned to FDA, Health Canada, EMA / EU, PMDA, and MFDS; SME training; post-inspection agency communication; gap assessments with targeted remediation plans.

Manufacturing Operations

Process and cleaning validation, equipment qualification (IQ / OQ / PQ), technology transfer, batch record review, stability studies, and supply chain qualification.

Quality Management

SOPs, training, risk management (FMEA), deviations, OOS / OOT, CAPA, change control, complaints, annual product review, data integrity, computer system validation, and quality system reviews.

PILLAR 03

GLP Auditing & Nonclinical Compliance

Nonclinical Laboratory Compliance (GLP)

GLP and OECD-aligned auditing, study oversight, and inspection-readiness for nonclinical test facilities and study sponsors.

Audits

Nonclinical laboratories (toxicology, bioanalytical, pathology, analytical, specialty labs), test facilities, study sponsors, contract research organizations, data and report reviews, and study-based audits.

Regulatory Inspections

Inspection readiness, preparation, and management; pre-inspection readiness reviews for FDA, EMA, and OECD-compliant programs; SME training; post-inspection agency communication.

Nonclinical Operations

Study plan and protocol review, dose formulation analysis, method validation, and GLP study-conduct monitoring.

Quality Management

SOPs, training, risk management, deviations, CAPA, change management, test-item characterization, facility / equipment validation and qualification, data integrity, QA statement audits, personnel training, and quality system evaluations.
